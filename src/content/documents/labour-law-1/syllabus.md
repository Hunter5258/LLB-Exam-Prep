## C. Detailed Topic Breakdown (Layer B — derived guidance)

> Each subsection below is **derived study guidance**. The "Official topic heading" field quotes the prescribed topic; the remaining fields are explanatory aids and introduce no new prescribed material.

### Unit U1 — Introduction to Labour and Industrial Law

#### U1-T01 — The Indian Trade Union Act, 1926
- **Official topic heading:** 1.1 The Indian Trade Union Act, 1926
- **Associated unit:** U1
- **Concepts to understand:** The Act as the foundational statute on trade unions; why a special statute was enacted for collective organisation of workmen; the Act's position alongside the other prescribed labour statutes.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** The Act by name and year of enactment (1926). *The syllabus states no section numbers; any section cited in an answer must be verified against the bare Act — see Section E and Section H.*
- **Legal distinctions:** Trade union as a collective body versus an individual workman; registered versus unregistered trade union (developed in U1-T03 and U1-T04).
- **Connected topics:** U1-T02 (history and objectives of trade unionism), U1-T03 (registration), U1-T04 (rights, immunities, amalgamation, dissolution), U1-T05 (penalties, collective bargaining, trade disputes).

#### U1-T02 — History of trade union movement in India, trade unionism, need, objects, types and functions
- **Official topic heading:** 1.2 History of trade union movement in India, trade unionism, need, objects, types and functions.
- **Associated unit:** U1
- **Concepts to understand:** Growth of the trade union movement in India; the meaning and purpose of trade unionism; why trade unions are needed; their objects; types of unions; functions performed by unions.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** No section number is stated. The topic is conceptual/historical and is anchored by the "objects" of a registered trade union, which the Act treats as a permitted object — verify against the bare Act before citing a provision.
- **Legal distinctions:** Need versus objects versus functions; types of trade unions (e.g., craft/industrial, general/occupational) — treat as classification explained in standard texts, not as a statutory classification in this syllabus.
- **Connected topics:** U1-T01 (the Act), U1-T03 (registration and advantages), U2-T04 (unfair labour practices), U2-T02 (collective outcomes such as strikes and lockouts).

#### U1-T03 — Registration of trade unions, rights and privileges of a registered trade union, advantages of registration
- **Official topic heading:** 1.3 Registration of trade unions, rights and privileges of a registered trade union, advantages of registration.
- **Associated unit:** U1
- **Concepts to understand:** The process of registration; the legal status conferred by registration; rights and privileges that flow from registration; the practical and legal advantages of registration.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Registration, rights and privileges of a registered trade union — named by subject matter only; no section numbers in the source. Verify before citing.
- **Legal distinctions:** Registered trade union versus unregistered association; "rights" versus "privileges" versus "advantages of registration"; registration as a status-conferring act rather than the creation of the union itself.
- **Connected topics:** U1-T01, U1-T02, U1-T04 (further rights, liabilities and immunities), and the referred case *Rangaswami and Another Vs. Registrar of Trade Unions, AIR 1962 Mad. 231*.

#### U1-T04 — Rights, privileges, liabilities and immunities of a registered trade union, amalgamation and dissolution of trade unions
- **Official topic heading:** 1.4 Rights, privileges, liabilities and immunities of a registered trade union, amalgamation and dissolution of trade unions.
- **Associated unit:** U1
- **Concepts to understand:** The full legal position of a registered trade union — rights, privileges, liabilities and immunities; the mechanisms of amalgamation and dissolution.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Rights, privileges, liabilities and immunities; amalgamation; dissolution — subject-matter only; no section numbers stated. Verify against the bare Act.
- **Legal distinctions:** Rights/privileges versus liabilities versus immunities; amalgamation (combination of unions) versus dissolution (ending of a union); the trade union's corporate-style capacities versus those of its office-bearers.
- **Connected topics:** U1-T03 (registration and advantages), U1-T05 (penalties and procedure), U1-T02 (objects and functions).

#### U1-T05 — Penalties and procedure, collective bargaining and trade disputes
- **Official topic heading:** 1.5 Penalties and procedure, collective bargaining and trade disputes
- **Associated unit:** U1
- **Concepts to understand:** Penalties and procedural machinery under the Act; the concept and importance of collective bargaining; trade disputes as the subject matter of collective bargaining.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Penalties and procedure under the Act; collective bargaining and trade disputes — no section numbers stated. Note that the detailed law of "industrial disputes" is separately prescribed in Unit U2; do not conflate the two.
- **Legal distinctions:** "Trade disputes" (this topic) versus "industrial dispute" as defined under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (U2-T01); penalties versus procedural provisions; collective bargaining versus adjudication.
- **Connected topics:** U2-T01 (industrial dispute vs. individual dispute), U2-T02 (strikes and lockouts), U2-T03 (reference to boards, courts or tribunals), U1-T04.

**Referred case (official, U1):** *Rangaswami and Another Vs. Registrar of Trade Unions, AIR 1962 Mad. 231* — study alongside U1-T03 (registration).

### Unit U2 — The Industrial Disputes Act, 1947

#### U2-T01 — Preliminary: appropriate government, industry, industrial dispute vs. individual dispute, public utility service, workman
- **Official topic heading:** 2.1 Preliminary : appropriate government, industry, industrial dispute vs. individual dispute, public utility service, workman.
- **Associated unit:** U2
- **Concepts to understand:** The preliminary definitions that determine the Act's application — appropriate government, industry, industrial dispute, individual dispute, public utility service and workman. These are threshold/ jurisdictional questions.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Definitions of "appropriate government", "industry", "industrial dispute", "public utility service" and "workman" — named by subject matter; the syllabus gives no section numbers. Verify before citing.
- **Legal distinctions:** Industrial dispute versus individual dispute; industry versus non-industry activity; appropriate government (central vs. state); public utility service versus other establishments; "workman" as a defined category.
- **Connected topics:** U2-T02 (consequences of a dispute — strikes, lockouts, lay-off, retrenchment), U2-T03 (reference machinery), U2-T04 (unfair labour practices), and the referred cases *Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board Vs. A. Rajappa* (industry), *Rohtas Industries Ltd. Vs. Rohtas Industries Staff Union* and the other U2 cases.

#### U2-T02 — Strikes and lockouts in industrial units, lay-off and retrenchment, compensation theory, special provisions relating to lay-off, retrenchment and closure in certain establishment
- **Official topic heading:** 2.2 Strikes and lockouts in industrial units, lay‐off and retrenchment, compensation theory, special provisions relating to lay‐off, retrenchment and closure in certain establishment.
- **Associated unit:** U2
- **Concepts to understand:** The law of strikes and lockouts in industrial units; lay-off, retrenchment and closure as distinct employment-terminating/interrupting concepts; the compensation theory underlying these provisions; special provisions for certain establishments.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Strikes, lockouts, lay-off, retrenchment, closure and related compensation — subject-matter only; no section numbers in the source. Verify against the bare Act.
- **Legal distinctions:** Strike versus lockout; lay-off versus retrenchment versus closure; lawful versus illegal strike/lockout; compensation payable on lay-off and retrenchment; general provisions versus special provisions for certain establishments.
- **Connected topics:** U2-T01 (definition of workman and industrial dispute), U2-T03 (adjudication), U2-T04 (unfair labour practices), referred case *Workmen of Dimakutri Tea Estate Vs. Dimakutri Tea Estate* (retrenchment).

#### U2-T03 — Reference of disputes to board, courts or tribunals
- **Official topic heading:** 2.3 Reference of disputes to board, courts or tribunals.
- **Associated unit:** U2
- **Concepts to understand:** The adjudicatory machinery — conciliation boards, courts and tribunals; reference of disputes by the appropriate government; the role and jurisdiction of each forum.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Reference of disputes to board, court or tribunal — no section numbers stated. Verify before citing.
- **Legal distinctions:** Board versus court versus tribunal; reference versus arbitration; adjudication (U2-T03) versus collective bargaining (U1-T05); appropriate government's role in making a reference (links to U2-T01).
- **Connected topics:** U2-T01, U2-T02, U2-T04; referred cases *Osmania University Vs. Industrial Tribunal* and *Rohtas Industries Ltd. Vs. Rohtas Industries Staff Union*.

#### U2-T04 — Un-fair labour practices, penalties and miscellaneous provisions
- **Official topic heading:** 2.4 Un‐fair labour practices, penalties and miscellaneous provisions.
- **Associated unit:** U2
- **Concepts to understand:** The concept of unfair labour practices; the penalties for contraventions; the miscellaneous provisions of the Act.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Unfair labour practices, penalties and miscellaneous provisions — subject-matter only; no section numbers stated. Verify against the bare Act (the Schedule of unfair labour practices is a key area to verify).
- **Legal distinctions:** Unfair labour practice versus illegal strike/lockout (U2-T02); penalties versus other remedies; employer's unfair labour practice versus workman's misconduct.
- **Connected topics:** U2-T01, U2-T02, U2-T03, U1-T05; referred case *Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board Vs. A. Rajappa*.

**Referred cases (official, U2):**
1. *Rohtas Industries Ltd. And another Vs. Rohtas Industries Staff Union and others AIR 1976, SC 425.*
2. *Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board Vs. A. Rajappa, 1978 (3) SCR 207*
3. *Osmania University Vs. Industrial Tribunal 1960, LLJ, A.P.*
4. *Workmen of Dimakutri Tea Estate Vs. Dimakutri Tea Estate, 1958, I, LLI. 500 SC*

### Unit U3 — The Factories Act, 1948

#### U3-T01 — Preliminary, manufacturing process, factory, occupier, the inspecting staff
- **Official topic heading:** 3.1 Preliminary, manufacturing process, factory, occupier, the inspecting staff.
- **Associated unit:** U3
- **Concepts to understand:** The Act's preliminary definitions — "manufacturing process", "factory", "occupier" — and the role of the inspecting staff. These determine whether the Act applies to a given premises and who is responsible for compliance.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Definitions of manufacturing process, factory and occupier, and the inspecting staff — no section numbers stated. Verify before citing.
- **Legal distinctions:** "Factory" versus other workplaces (e.g., shops and establishments under U5); "manufacturing process" as a threshold test; occupier versus manager/owner; preliminary definitions versus substantive obligations (U3-T02 to U3-T05).
- **Connected topics:** U3-T02 to U3-T05 (all substantive obligations); U5 (Assam Shops and Establishment Act, 1961 — a different, state-level sphere); referred case *Lal Mohammad and other Vs. Indian Railway Construction Co. Ltd.* (factory/occupier).

#### U3-T02 — Health and cleanliness measures, safety arrangement measures in factory
- **Official topic heading:** 3.2 Health and cleanliness measures, safety arrangement measures in factory.
- **Associated unit:** U3
- **Concepts to understand:** Statutory duties concerning health and cleanliness in factories; safety arrangements and devices; the employer's/occupier's obligation to maintain a safe and healthy workplace.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Health and cleanliness measures and safety arrangements — named by subject matter; no section numbers in the source. Verify against the bare Act.
- **Legal distinctions:** Health measures versus safety measures; cleanliness versus general maintenance; duties of the occupier versus rights of the worker; preventive safety obligations versus compensation after injury (contrast U4-T01/U4-T02).
- **Connected topics:** U3-T01 (threshold definitions), U3-T03 (hazardous processes and welfare), U4 (Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 — compensation once injury occurs).

#### U3-T03 — Provisions relating to hazardous processes, welfare of workers
- **Official topic heading:** 3.3 Provisions relating to hazardous processes, welfare of workers.
- **Associated unit:** U3
- **Concepts to understand:** Special provisions for hazardous processes; welfare facilities and measures for workers; the Act's preventive and protective dimension.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Hazardous processes and welfare of workers — no section numbers stated. Verify before citing; the hazardous-processes chapter is a distinct statutory segment.
- **Legal distinctions:** Hazardous processes versus ordinary manufacturing processes; welfare provisions versus health/safety provisions (U3-T02); preventive regulation versus compensation (U4).
- **Connected topics:** U3-T02, U3-T05, U4-T01/U4-T02; the Asiad and Bonded Labour cases listed under U4 are outside this unit and must not be attributed to it.

#### U3-T04 — Working hours of adults, employment of young persons, employment of women
- **Official topic heading:** 3.4 Working hours of adults, employment of young persons, employment of women.
- **Associated unit:** U3
- **Concepts to understand:** Regulation of working hours for adult workers; restrictions on the employment of young persons and women; night work, rest intervals and related protections.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Working hours of adults, employment of young persons, employment of women — subject-matter only; no section numbers stated. Verify against the bare Act.
- **Legal distinctions:** Adult worker versus young person versus woman worker; hours of work versus overtime; employment restrictions versus wage entitlements (U3-T05 annual leave with wages; U4-T03/U4-T04 minimum wages).
- **Connected topics:** U3-T05 (annual leave with wages), U5-T02 (employment of children and women under the Assam Act), U4-T03/U4-T04 (wage regulation).

#### U3-T05 — Annual leave with wages, special provisions, penalties and procedures
- **Official topic heading:** 3.5 Annual leave with wages, special provisions, penalties and procedures.
- **Associated unit:** U3
- **Concepts to understand:** The entitlement to annual leave with wages; special provisions of the Act; penalties and procedural machinery for enforcement.
- **Important statutory provisions (identifiable from source):** Annual leave with wages, special provisions, penalties and procedure — no section numbers stated. Verify before citing.
- **Legal distinctions:** Annual leave with wages versus other leave; special provisions versus general provisions; penalties versus procedure; statutory leave entitlement (U3-T05) versus wages regulation under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (U4-T03/U4-T04).
- **Connected topics:** U3-T01 to U3-T04, U4-T03/U4-T04, U5-T01 (leave and wages under the Assam Act).

**Referred case (official, U3):** *Lal Mohammad and other Vs. Indian Railway Construction Co. Ltd. & Others, (1999) I, SSC 596.*

### Unit U4 — Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 and The Minimum Wages Act, 1948

- **Connected topics:** U4-T04 (fixation/revision and claims), U5-T01 (hours of work, leave and wages under the Assam Act), U3-T05 (annual leave with wages); referred cases *People's Union for Democratic Rights Vs. - **Connected topics:** U3-T01/U3-T05, U5-T01, U5-T02.

### Unit U1 — Introduction to Labour and Industrial Law

**U1-T01 — The Indian Trade Union Act, 1926**
- **Unit:** U1
- **Core concepts:** The Act as the foundation of trade-union law; legislative purpose; place among prescribed labour statutes.
- **Relevant statutory provisions:** The Indian Trade Union Act, 1926 (section numbers not in syllabus — verify).
- **Important definitions and legal principles:** A trade union is a collective association of workmen formed for prescribed objects; registration gives legal status.
- **Suggested answer-preparation approach:** Open with the Act's role in recognising collective organisation; give the year and objective; then lead into the topics that follow (history, registration, rights).
- **Revision status:** Not started.

**U1-T02 — History, trade unionism, need, objects, types and functions**

## F. Answer-Writing Preparation (Layer B — derived guidance)

Use formal Indian legal academic English. Structure every answer with a clear introduction, body and conclusion; state the statutory position first, then authority, then application.

### 1. Short-answer questions
- Identify exactly what is asked (definition, concept, case, or provision) and answer that point directly.
- State the statutory source by Act name; add a section number only if verified.
- Keep to the precise scope of the prescribed topic — do not wander into adjacent units.
- Example approach: "Under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, an 'industrial dispute' is distinguished from an individual dispute by ..." (then complete the distinction, U2-T01).

### 2. Medium-length explanatory answers
- Use a three-part format: (i) statutory framework, (ii) the prescribed sub-topics in their official sequence, (iii) a short concluding line.
- Follow the order of the syllabus subtopics; this demonstrates complete coverage.
- Where the syllabus lists more than one element (e.g., "rights, privileges, liabilities and immunities" in U1-T04), deal with each in a separate paragraph.

### 3. Long analytical answers
- Open with the object and scope of the statute (e.g., U4-T01 for the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923).
- Develop each prescribed subtopic under its own heading.
- Integrate the referred case(s) listed for that unit, stating the proposition each is cited for — after confirming the proposition from an authoritative text, since the syllabus gives only citations, not holdings.
- Close with a balanced evaluation, e.g., prevention under the Factories Act (U3-T02/U3-T03) versus compensation under the 1923 Act (U4-T01/U4-T02).

### 4. Statutory interpretation questions
- Begin with the exact statutory language (quote the definition where known).
- Explain the interpretive issue, apply the relevant judicial authority from the unit's referred cases, and state the resulting legal position.
- Keep the distinction between the **prescribed** provision and the **derived** explanation explicit: do not present an inferred section number as if printed in the syllabus.
- Confirm any section number against the bare Act before writing it in the answer.

### 5. Distinction and comparison questions
- Use a consistent two-column or point-by-point format: basis of distinction, then the position of each concept, then a one-line conclusion.
- Comparisons most directly supported by the syllabus:
  - Industrial dispute vs. individual dispute (U2-T01)
  - Strike vs. lockout; lay-off vs. retrenchment vs. closure (U2-T02)
  - Registered vs. unregistered trade union (U1-T03)
  - Rights/privileges vs. liabilities vs. immunities (U1-T04)
  - Factory (U3-T01) vs. shop/establishment (U5-T01) — central vs. state legislation
  - "Wages" under the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (U4-T03) vs. annual leave with wages under the Factories Act, 1948 (U3-T05)
  - Prevention/health-and-safety duties (U3-T02/U3-T03) vs. compensation liability (U4-T01/U4-T02)
- Do not invent a comparison where both sides are not within the syllabus.
